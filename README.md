## 🕹️ About

STUNTBOOST is a fast-paced action racing game by Byting Games, released on September 22, 2026. Set across imaginative cardboard tracks built around a home, it puts you on a fingerboard with one goal: finish each course as quickly as possible. Build and preserve momentum through leaps, grinds, flips, gravity control, downhill dashes, shortcuts, and collectible hunts. Medals, unlockable rooms, online leaderboards, and precise time attacks make every run an opportunity to improve.

STUNTBOOST is made for players who enjoy arcade racing, skateboarding movement, speedrunning, and mastering demanding time trials.

## ✨ Features

- 🏠 **Cardboard Worlds** — Explore imaginative miniature environments spread across rooms in a home.
- ⚡ **Momentum-Based Movement** — Use slopes, dives, dashes, and controlled landings to maintain maximum speed.
- 🏆 **Online Leaderboards** — Compare level times with friends and compete for the top position.
- 🏅 **Medal Challenges** — Replay levels to refine your lines and earn medals based on performance.
- 🛹 **Fingerboard Racing** — Race through skate-inspired courses designed for speed, flow, and precision.
- 🤸 **Stunts and Tricks** — Perform leaps, grinds, and flips while searching for the fastest route.
